Privacy notice

What we collect and who can see it

We collect: your name, email, role and department; faculty scheduling data (specializations, unit limits, availability, preferences, leave dates); saved schedules; and an audit log of who did what and when (ids and counts only).

We never collect: student names, student IDs or grades, your password (a secure sign-in service handles it and we never see it), or IP tracking. Our hosting providers may keep standard server logs.

Who can see it: a chairperson sees only their own department. Faculty see their own preferences and leave and their department's approved schedule. Developers (admin) can see everything for maintenance.
