About

ATLAS PSU
Version 1.0 · Thesis prototype
What it is

ATLAS PSU: A Teaching Load Assignment and Management System for Department Chairpersons of the College of Sciences at Palawan State University.

It recommends faculty teaching loads. Chairpersons review, edit and finalize every assignment, and faculty members see the finished schedule.

The problem
  • Teaching loads are assigned by hand with spreadsheets.
  • The existing college tool only plots schedules. It does not assign, check constraints or resolve conflicts.
  • Matching specialization to subjects takes the most time.
  • Time, room and subject preferences, class sizes and block scheduling are not handled.
  • Fairness of the result is hard to confirm.
Purpose

Generate optimized faculty-to-subject assignments from specialization, availability and CHED load limits, then give chairpersons workload charts and a fairness report to decide with.

How it works
  1. CP-SAT solver assigns professors under the hard rules and balances the load.
  2. Genetic algorithm improves times and rooms for preferences and compactness without changing any professor.
  3. Analytics measure utilization, fairness and specialization match.
  4. Review: edit assignments, then export to CSV or PDF.
Who it is for

Department chairpersons of Information Technology, Computer Science, Medical Biology, Marine Biology and Environmental Science. Faculty members have a read-only view of their own schedule and the department schedule.

Where it comes from

Palawan State University, Puerto Princesa City, Palawan. A capstone thesis project of Computer Studies, built from April to July 2026.

Structured interviews with the Information Technology and Computer Science chairpersons on June 8, 2026 shaped the design. Their clearest request: help with decisions, not a system that decides alone.

Who made it

Arthur V. Baldosano Jr., sole full-stack developer and researcher: requirements, system design, the Flask backend, the scheduling engine and this interface.

Status
  • Proposal prototype defense completed. Final thesis defense ahead.
  • Data is held in memory and files. A Postgres database is planned.
  • Not yet piloted by a department chairperson.
Technology

Python, Flask, Google OR-Tools CP-SAT, a custom genetic algorithm, vanilla JavaScript, Chart.js. Installable as a web app.

Your data

You sign in with your PSU account through Supabase Auth. Your role and department come from your profile, and the server checks them on every request. Finished runs are saved to the database as drafts until the chairperson saves or approves them. Some working data and settings also stay in this browser.

Privacy notice

We collect:

  • Your name, email, role and department.
  • Faculty scheduling data: specializations, unit limits, availability, preferences and leave dates.
  • Saved schedules.
  • An audit log of who did what and when (ids and counts only, never schedule content or personal data).

We never collect:

  • Student names, student IDs or grades.
  • Your password. Supabase Auth handles sign in and we never see it.
  • IP tracking. Our hosting providers may keep standard server logs.

Who can see it:

  • A chairperson sees only their own department.
  • Faculty see their own preferences and leave, and the approved schedule of their department.
  • Developers (admin) can see everything for maintenance.
